# disregard - пренебрегать

1. disregard the rules
2. disregard the warning
3. disregard the fact
4. disregard the advice
5. disregard the evidence
6. disregard the order
7. disregard the consequences

- I have disregarded the warning about the storm, and now I am stuck outside.
- He disregarded the rules during the match last night and received a red card.
- She was disregarding the advice of her teacher while she was writing the essay.
- By the time the manager arrived, the team had already disregarded the safety order.
