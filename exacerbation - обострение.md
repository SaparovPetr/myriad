# exacerbation - обострение

1. severe exacerbation
2. acute exacerbation
3. exacerbation of symptoms
4. chronic exacerbation
5. exacerbation of disease
6. exacerbation of pain
7. sudden exacerbation

- I have had a severe exacerbation of my asthma this month, so I have been using my inhaler more often.
- Last winter, she experienced an acute exacerbation of her arthritis and could not walk without help.
- At that moment, the doctors were treating an exacerbation of symptoms in the patient, while the nurse prepared the oxygen mask.
- By the time the ambulance arrived, the exacerbation of his chronic bronchitis had already caused a dangerous drop in oxygen levels, and he had lost consciousness.
