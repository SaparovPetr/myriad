3. increasingly popular
4. increasingly common

- Past Simple: Last year, the problem became increasingly difficult for the local government to solve.
- Past Continuous: While the team was working on the project, the deadline was becoming increasingly unrealistic.
- Past Perfect: By the time the board made its final decision, the situation had already become increasingly complicated.
