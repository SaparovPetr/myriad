# proliferate - разрастаться

1. proliferate rapidly
2. proliferate widely
3. proliferate uncontrollably
4. proliferate throughout
5. proliferate in
6. proliferate across
7. proliferate among

- Present Perfect: Online courses have proliferated rapidly over the past few years.
- Past Simple: Fake news proliferated widely during the election campaign.
- Past Continuous: Weeds were proliferating uncontrollably in the garden all summer.
- Past Perfect: By the time the authorities acted, the rumors had already proliferated throughout the city.
