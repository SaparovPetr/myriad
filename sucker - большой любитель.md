# sucker - большой любитель

1. a sucker for punishment
2. a sucker for romance
3. a sucker for a good story
4. a sucker for chocolate
5. a sucker for a bargain
6. a sucker for nostalgia

- Present Perfect: I have been a sucker for old horror movies since I was a teenager.
- Past Simple: Last weekend, she was a sucker for that cheesy love song and played it ten times.
- Past Continuous: He was being a sucker for every flashy advertisement while we were walking through the mall yesterday.
- Past Perfect: By the time the sale ended, I had already been a sucker for three unnecessary gadgets.
