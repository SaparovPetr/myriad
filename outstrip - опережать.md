# outstrip - опережать

1. outstrip demand
2. outstrip supply
3. outstrip growth
4. outstrip expectations
5. outstrip rivals
6. outstrip inflation
7. outstrip capacity

- Present Perfect: The company has outstripped its main competitor in the last two quarters.
- Past Simple: The new technology outstripped all previous versions in speed and efficiency.
- Past Continuous: While the market was outstripping production, the factory was struggling to hire more workers.
- Past Perfect: By the time the manager arrived, the sales team had already outstripped the annual target.
