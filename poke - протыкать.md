# poke - протыкать

1. Poke a hole
2. poke a finger
3. poke a stick
4. poke a needle
5. poke a hole in something
6. poke through
7. poke out

- She has just poked a hole in the paper with her pencil.

- I have poked a hole in the paper with my pen.
- He poked a hole in the balloon and it popped.
- She was poking the dough with a fork to let the steam out.
- They had poked a hole in the fence before the guard arrived.
