# plausibly - правдоподобно

1. plausibly argue
2. plausibly explain
3. plausibly claim
4. plausibly deny
5. plausibly suggest
6. plausibly imagine
7. plausibly account for

- Present Perfect: She has plausibly argued that the data was misinterpreted.
- Past Simple: He plausibly explained his absence to the teacher yesterday.
- Past Continuous: While the detective was interviewing him, the suspect was plausibly denying any involvement.
- Past Perfect: By the time the report was published, the scientist had plausibly accounted for all the anomalies in the experiment.
