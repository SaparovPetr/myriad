# notorious - печально известный

1. notoriously difficult
2. notoriously unreliable
3. notoriously bad
4. notoriously slow
5. notoriously hard to please
6. notoriously private
7. notoriously expensive

Examples:
- Present Perfect: He has been notoriously bad at keeping promises since he started his own company.
- Past Simple: The restaurant was notoriously slow last summer, so we stopped going there.
- Past Continuous: While we were waiting, the service was notoriously unreliable, and the manager kept apologizing.
- Past Perfect: By the time we finally got our food, the kitchen had been notoriously hard to please for over an hour, and two tables had already left.
