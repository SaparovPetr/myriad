# compulsion - навязчивая привычка

1. irresistible compulsion
2. strong compulsion
3. overpowering compulsion
4. sudden compulsion
5. uncontrollable compulsion
6. inner compulsion
7. strange compulsion

- I have felt a strange compulsion to check the door three times before leaving.
- She felt a sudden compulsion to count the steps as she walked upstairs.
- He was fighting a strong compulsion to wash his hands again while the meeting was going on.
- They had felt an irresistible compulsion to arrange the objects on the desk before anyone noticed.
