# dig - сходить с ума

1. dig deep
2. dig in
3. dig out
4. dig up
5. dig into
6. dig through
7. dig for

- I have dug deep to understand why he left without a word.
- Yesterday she dug in her heels and refused to change the plan.
- At noon, they were digging out old photos from the attic.
- By the time the meeting ended, he had dug up the real reason for the delay.
