# worship - поклонение

1. worship God
2. worship idols
3. worship the ground
4. worship at the altar
5. worship in silence
6. worship together
7. worship service

- Present Perfect: She has worshipped the ground he walks on since they first met.
- Past Simple: The ancient tribe worshipped idols made of stone and wood.
- Past Continuous: While the choir was singing, the congregation was worshipping in silence.
- Past Perfect: By the time the priest arrived, the faithful had already worshipped together at the altar.
