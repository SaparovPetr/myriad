# utter - произносить

1. utter a word
2. utter a sound
3. utter a cry
4. utter a sigh
5. utter a lie
6. utter a prayer
7. utter a threat

- She has never uttered a word about her past to anyone.
- He uttered a sound of surprise when he saw the gift.
- While she was waiting for the news, she uttered a soft cry of relief.
- By the time the police arrived, the witness had already uttered a complete lie to the reporter.
