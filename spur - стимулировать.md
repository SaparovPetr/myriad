# spur - стимулировать

1. spur growth
2. spur development
3. spur innovation
4. spur demand
5. spur investment
6. spur action
7. spur change

Example sentences:

- Present Perfect: The new policy has spurred growth in the tech sector.
- Past Simple: The crisis spurred action from the local government.
- Past Continuous: The manager was spurring development when the budget cuts were announced.
- Past Perfect: By the time the report was published, the data had already spurred innovation.
