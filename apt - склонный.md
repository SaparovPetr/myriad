# apt - склонный

1. apt to forget
2. apt to be
3. apt to happen
4. apt to make mistakes
5. apt to change
6. apt to believe
7. apt to get angry

- Present Perfect: He has been apt to forget names since childhood.
- Past Simple: She was apt to make mistakes under pressure.
- Past Continuous: They were apt to change their plans whenever it rained.
- Past Perfect: I had been apt to believe rumors before I learned the truth.
