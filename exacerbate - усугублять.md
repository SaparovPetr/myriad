# exacerbate - усугублять

1. exacerbate the problem
2. exacerbate the situation
3. exacerbate the crisis
4. exacerbate tensions
5. exacerbate symptoms
6. exacerbate the conflict
7. exacerbate the impact

- I have exacerbated the problem by ignoring the warning signs.
- She exacerbated the situation when she shouted at the manager.
- The noise from the construction site was exacerbating my headache all afternoon.
- By the time we arrived, the delay had exacerbated the crisis beyond control.
