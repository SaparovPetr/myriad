# figment - вымысел

1. figment of imagination
2. figment of fantasy
3. figment of dream
4. figment of myth
5. figment of memory
6. figment of rumor
7. figment of fear

- Present Perfect: She has always dismissed the story as a figment of imagination.
- Past Simple: He claimed the ghost was a figment of fantasy.
- Past Continuous: While they were searching, the rumor was spreading that the treasure was a figment of myth.
- Past Perfect: By the time the investigation ended, the investigators had realized the evidence was a figment of memory.
