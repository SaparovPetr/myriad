# outrage - негодование

1. public outrage
2. moral outrage
3. rightful outrage
4. wave of outrage
5. spark outrage
6. express outrage
7. cause outrage

- Present Perfect: The new policy has sparked public outrage across the city.
- Past Simple: The scandal caused a wave of moral outrage last year.
- Past Continuous: The citizens were expressing their outrage at the town hall meeting when the mayor arrived.
- Past Perfect: By the time the company apologized, the public had already expressed rightful outrage for weeks.
