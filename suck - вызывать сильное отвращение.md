# suck - вызывать сильное отвращение

1. suck at
2. suck up to
3. suck the life out of
4. suck it up
5. suck on
6. suck in
7. suck out

- Present Perfect: I have sucked at cooking since I moved out, so I order dinner every night.
- Past Simple: She sucked up to the manager yesterday, but everyone noticed it.
- Past Continuous: He was sucking on a lemon while his brother told a bad joke.
- Past Perfect: By the time I arrived, the vacuum had sucked in all the dust from the carpet.
